Damaged, incorrect or poor-quality items

If your delivery arrives damaged, incomplete, incorrect, or not as described, contact us as soon as you can. We will assess the issue and explain the available remedy under applicable law. Do not consume food you believe is unsafe. Statutory rights are not reduced by this policy.

03

Change of mind

For eligible distance purchases, a statutory withdrawal period may apply. In the EU this is generally 14 days after delivery. Exceptions can apply to rapidly perishable goods and certain unsealed goods unsuitable for return for health or hygiene reasons. An exception to change-of-mind returns does not remove rights relating to faulty goods. [CONFIRM THE RULES FOR EACH PRODUCT CATEGORY.]
